User manual · version 0.2.0

CSEN

ResoOpus

A drone synthesizer for cinematic, ambient and experimental soundscapes. Four hand-tuned, microtonal voices in five types play as soon as the plugin is on a track. In the centre sits the GRAVITY knob, which leads from pure tuning to chaos. Add a polyphonic LEAD with a filter per note, two filters with routing, an echo, a space and a MOD page with two LFOs, a random source, a modulation envelope and a modulation matrix. Play the drone only, the lead only, or both.

VST3 · Windows / macOSAU · macOSinstrument · drone + lead (up to 8 notes)Ableton Live 11 / 124 voices · 5 types · 24 modulations · 70 presets

01How it works

ResoOpus is an instrument that plays by itself. Every voice has a HOLD level it sounds at without being touched. Playing a voice lifts it above that level, and letting go returns it there. The keys don't trigger the drone, they shape it.

PageContents

LEADThe melodic layer: oscillators, a filter with its envelope per note, amp envelope, polyphony, glide, vibrato, ARP.
FXFILTER A and B with routing, ECHO, MOTION and SPACE.
MODLFO 1, LFO 2, RANDOM, MOD ENV and the matrix of all modulations.
4 voices⇄GRAVITY · DUST+LEAD (1–8 notes)→FILTER A / B→MOTION→ECHO→SPACE→MASTER → limiter

Drone, lead, or both

The DRONE and LEAD tabs in the top bar carry power switches (gold = playing):

DRONELEADResult
onoffDrone only (default). The plates C3–F3 play the voices, other keys do nothing.
ononBoth. The plates play the drone, every other key plays the LEAD.
offonLead only. The drone voices fall silent and use no CPU, every key including the plates plays the LEAD. The DRONE page is dimmed.

Voices, LEAD and filters run oversampled (2× by default), so high notes and heavy modulation stay clean. A soft limiter at the end keeps the output below −0.3 dBFS.

Silent? That's fine.Some presets (Slow Bloom, Requiem Air, Four Plates) have HOLD at zero and only sound while you play. All other presets drone right away.

02Quick start

  1. Put ResoOpus on a MIDI track in Ableton (Plug-Ins → Gavr → ResoOpus). You hear a drone immediately.
  2. Play the white keys C3, D3, E3, F3 (numbered 1–4 on the keyboard below). Each one swells one voice, more the harder you play.
  3. Turn GRAVITY in the middle. To the left: pure tuning and slow beating. To the right the voices start to tremble, growl and finally pulse on their own.
  4. Click the preset name at the top. The browser opens with categories and a description of what to turn first.
  5. Switch the LEAD on at its tab: keys outside C3–F3 then play a melody, in Poly 8 chords as well.

03Voices

Every voice is its own module. Click it to select it and its details appear in the VOICE panel below.

  • The header shows the type and voice number (= plate number on the keyboard). The dot lights up while the voice sounds.
  • TUNE: C0–C8, free and microtonal. Double-click to type a note with cents, e.g. D2+12.
  • The second knob follows the type: TIDE (CHOIR), FM (SIREN), SHARP (PULSAR), INDEX (QUASAR), POSITION (NEBULA).
  • HOLD: drone level without touching anything. 0 % = only sounds while played.
  • PAN: position left / right.
  • SCOPE: the live signal of the voice in its type colour, always two periods of its note so the picture stands still. The height is the level. Click to switch to SPECTRUM (harmonics, chord, TIDE growl).
  • VOL: drag for volume (−60 to +6 dB), double-click = −2 dB. The thin line below is the live level, the tick on it is HOLD.

The voice detail always has TYPE, CHARACTER (see below), ATTACK (1 ms–30 s, how slowly the voice swells, also when HOLD brings it in) and RELEASE (1 ms–30 s, how long it fades). A voice set to OFF uses no CPU.

04Voice types

Every type has its own waveform icon in its colour in the TYPE switch: CHOIR (five saws), SIREN (a square with a siren), PULSAR (triangle to square), QUASAR (FM) and NEBULA (wavetable).

CHOIR: five saws as a chord

  • CHORD fills all five intervals at once: Unison (a few cents apart, beating), Octaves, Fifths, Major (0 4 7 12 16), Minor (0 3 7 12 15).
  • OSC 1–5: interval of each saw above TUNE (−24 to +36 semitones). Shift-drag moves in cents. The LED above the knob switches the saw off.
  • TIDE (in the voice module): up to 50 % it pulls the whole chord down. Beyond 50 % it sinks further and the saws start to modulate each other: growl, metallic overtones and crackle at the bottom (the saws drop to a few Hz and click). Tame the crackle with GRIT in MASTER.

SIREN: sirens, birds, monsters, wind

ControlWhat it does
FM (voice module)How far the modulator sweeps the pitch, up to ±3 octaves.
RATEModulator speed 0.05–300 Hz: slow wails, warbles, buzzing. Above ~30 Hz it becomes a new timbre.
AMHow much the modulator chops the tone into beeps (Morse, alarm).
DIVIDERAdds a sub-harmonic: 1:2 = an octave below, 1:3, 1:5… up to 1:16. Growl and monsters.
NOISEWhite noise: wind, surf, static.
WAVEModulator shape: square (two-tone siren) to triangle (wail).

PULSAR: one tone that blooms

  • SHARP (voice module): the shape of the tone from a soft triangle through shark fin and saw to a sharp square.
  • SYMMETRY: the symmetry of the shape. For the square it is the pulse width: a narrow pulse sounds thin and nasal, 50 % is a pure square.
  • BLOOM: the envelope bends the tone. It starts a little flat and dull and blooms into pitch and edge.
  • TWIN and BLEND: a second tone above the first (−12 to +24 semitones). A few cents off unison = slow beating, 7 = a fifth.
  • SUB: a soft tone an octave below. VIBRATO: up to 50 cents, every voice at a slightly different speed.

QUASAR: two-operator FM

Two sine oscillators: the modulator moves the phase of the tone. Bells, metal, electric piano, digital pads.

  • INDEX (voice module): depth of the FM, from a pure sine to a bright, edgy tone.
  • RATIO: frequency of the modulator against the tone (0.5–8). Whole numbers sound harmonic, others (1.41, 3.5…) like bells and metal.
  • FINE: detunes the modulator by ±50 cents. Slow beating and shimmer.
  • FEEDBACK: the modulator modulates itself. The sine turns towards a saw and noise.
  • TRACK: how much INDEX follows the level of the voice. A quiet voice is darker, so a fading bell naturally darkens.

The FM depth is limited so the sidebands stay below Nyquist. Even high notes with a big INDEX don't alias (−76 dB at 1976 Hz).

NEBULA: wavetables

  • TABLE: three families of eight waves each. Vocal (hum and the vowels U, O, A, AE, E, I, ER), Organ (stops from a flute to the full organ and a reed), Glass (glassy, bell-like and digital spectra).
  • POSITION (voice module): where in the table you are. It moves smoothly between the waves.
  • SCAN: the level of the voice moves POSITION, so playing a plate walks through the table (negative = the other way).
  • DRIFT: POSITION wanders slowly on its own.

CHARACTER: Digital – Clean – Analog

A continuous knob for every type. Centre: clean. Right (Analog): every oscillator a little detuned and drifting on its own, rounder edges, even harmonics and gentle saturation. Left (Digital): fewer bits and a lower sample rate, aliasing on purpose as a colour (old samplers and game consoles).

The CHOIR saw and the SIREN square are band-limited: their jumps are smoothed (BLEP). QUASAR watches the FM bandwidth and NEBULA has a band-limited copy of every table per octave. The voices also run oversampled, so digital aliasing only appears when you ask for it (CHARACTER Digital).

05GRAVITY

GRAVITY is the heart of the instrument: one knob from tuning to chaos. The voices frequency-modulate each other, first in pairs, then in one ring, and finally the instrument's own output joins the loop.

STILL 0–25 %Pure tuning, only slow beating between the voices.
ORBIT 25–55 %The voices of a pair pull on each other (FM), the tone trembles and glitters.
TURBULENCE 55–85 %All four in one loop, the output feeds back into the voices. It growls and breathes.
COLLAPSE 85–100 %The loop runs away and pulses on its own. It never repeats.

The coloured dots on the orbit are the voices in their type colour; as GRAVITY rises they pull together and shake. The top corners show X/Y figures of the pairs 1-2 and 3-4: a voice against its partner. In STILL a calm, slowly turning figure, in ORBIT it trembles, in TURBULENCE it falls apart. Loudness changes by only about 2 dB between STILL and COLLAPSE, so you can automate GRAVITY without jumps.

  • PATH (select button on the right of the GRAVITY header): Auto (pairs up to the middle, then a ring), Pairs (always pairs 1·2 and 3·4), Ring (always the ring 1→2→3→4→1). Right-click the GRAVITY knob for Modulate with.
  • Drag up / down (Shift = fine), mouse wheel, double-click = type a value, Cmd/Ctrl-click = default 15 %.

When GRAVITY is modulated, a ring appears around the knob with a dot for its live value. Drag the ring to change the depth.

The best pairing is finger pressure (aftertouch) on GRAVITY: a light touch keeps the voices in orbit, pressing harder pulls them into turbulence (presets Gravity Well, Event Horizon). Or an LFO in tempo that swings GRAVITY over bars (preset Lunar Tide).

ECHO TO VOICES on the FX page feeds the echo back into the voices as extra FM. With more GRAVITY the whole instrument becomes one loop.

06MASTER and DUST

  • PITCH: transposes all four voices together (±24 semitones), their intervals stay the same.
  • DUST: analog life for the whole instrument. Slow drift of the tuning, voices leaking into each other, a quiet breath of noise (at most −54 dBFS). Unlike CHARACTER it works on all voices and on how they relate.
  • VOLUME: output level (−∞ to +12 dB).
  • GRIT: how sharp the edges are that the voices modulate each other with (GRAVITY, DUST, TIDE). 100 % = raw growl and crackle. Lower rounds the edges and the crackle goes: at 50 % the clicks of saws pulled down to a few Hz by TIDE disappear while a normal tone barely changes, at 0 % even COLLAPSE gets smoother.
  • FILL (select button in the header): spreads the four voices over a chosen scale from a chosen root.

07LEAD page

LEAD is the melodic layer above the drone (or instead of it). Switch it on at its tab. It goes through the same FILTER, ECHO and SPACE as the voices; with ROUTING Split it gets its own FILTER B.

PanelControls
LEAD OSCWAVE morphs sine → triangle → shark fin → saw → square (icons around the knob). OSC 2 interval ±12 semitones, OSC 2 LEVEL, SYMMETRY (pulse width), OCTAVE ±2, SUB sine an octave below, SYNC = hard sync of OSC 2.
LEAD FILTERA filter per note: CUTOFF, RESONANCE, ENV (how far the envelope moves the cutoff, ±6 octaves), KEY (the cutoff follows the note), MODEL (the same seven as FILTER; COMB with KEY 100 % and CUTOFF 262 Hz = C3 plays an in-tune plucked string) and MODE (low, band, high pass). At 20 kHz with no envelope it is bypassed and colours nothing.
FILTER ENVADSR of the filter. The coloured pill in the header is the LEAD ENV source: drag it onto a knob and the lead envelope modulates anything (GRAVITY, say).
AMP ENVADSR of the level: ATTACK 1 ms–10 s, DECAY and RELEASE 1 ms–30 s, SUSTAIN.
LEAD VOICEVOICES: Mono (every note restarts the envelopes), Legato (overlapping notes only slide), Poly 4 and Poly 8 (chords and melodies over the drone). GLIDE, VELOCITY (how much the strike sets level and filter envelope), VIBRATO, VIB RATE, VIB DELAY, TOUCH VIB, VOLUME, PAN.
ARPARP, LATCH, DIRECTION Up / Down / Ping-Pong / Random, RATE 1/4 to 1/32 including triplets, OCTAVES 1–4. Locked to the song tempo.

In Mono and Legato the last pressed note plays; release it and the LEAD returns to the previously held note. In Poly a new note takes a free voice, else the quietest releasing one, else the oldest. The sustain pedal holds notes until you let go of it.

08FX page

FILTER A and FILTER B

Two stereo filters for the whole instrument, running oversampled. Both have CUTOFF 20 Hz–20 kHz, RESONANCE 0–10, DRIVE 0–10, MODEL and MODE (low, band, high pass, with icons). SPREAD on A detunes the left and right filters against each other. FILTER B is switched on with ON.

Between the filters sits the ROUTING panel. Its graph is the real response of both filters, measured from the same code that plays, modulation included: when an LFO swings the cutoff, the curve moves. Gold is A, violet is B, white is the result of the routing (Serial = A × B, Parallel = A + B). Split and Stereo label the curves DRONE / LEAD or LEFT / RIGHT. Below the graph: the ROUTING switch and a diagram of where the signal flows.

ROUTINGWhat it does
SerialA, then B. A low pass on A + a high pass on B = a band.
ParallelA and B side by side, summed. Two characters at once, or a notch.
SplitThe drone through A, the LEAD through B. A dark drone and a bright lead that don't muddy each other.
StereoA on the left, B on the right. A wide, moving image.
ModelSlopeCharacter
GROWL12 dBAn op-amp state variable whose integrators clip. Keeps the lows at any resonance. DRIVE bends the wave unevenly (even harmonics, a growl). At the top of the resonance it rings on its own with a gritty tone.
LADDER24 dBTransistor ladder: round and warm. The lows thin out with resonance (about 7 dB), at the top it rings as a clean sine. DRIVE adds odd harmonics.
DIODE24 dBDiode ladder: a rubbery resonance that squeezes with the level and takes the lows. The diodes clip unevenly (even and odd harmonics). It does not ring on its own; the resonance bites more than it rings.
SCREAM12 dBSallen-Key with diodes in the resonance: aggressive, bites even more with drive. From resonance ~9.3 it screams and rings with a harsh, almost square tone. DRIVE clips the hardest.
SILK12 dBA clean digital filter: linear, the resonance does not depend on the level and adds no harmonics. Very narrow, but it never rings on its own. DRIVE only pushes into a soft ceiling.
COMBtunedComb filter: CUTOFF is its pitch, RESONANCE its feedback. MODE LOW = a damped string (warm), BAND = every harmonic rings (metal), HIGH = only the odd harmonics an octave lower (a hollow tube, a clarinet). On the LEAD with KEY 100 % and CUTOFF 262 Hz it plays an in-tune plucked string.
VOWELformantsThree formants like a throat and mouth: CUTOFF walks the vowels U–O–A–E–I (dark to bright), RESONANCE sharpens them, MODE is the size of the throat: LOW man, BAND woman, HIGH child.

At full resonance Band Pass keeps its level: a narrow band doesn't vanish, it sings. The LEAD filter has the same seven models.

ECHO

Five modes (MODE) on the same knobs. The knob names change with what they do in each mode, and a short line at the bottom of the panel says what the mode does (as in MOTION and SPACE).

MODETIME 1 · TIME 2 · MODSound
DUALTIME 1 · TIME 2 · MODTwo lines, left and right, with cross feedback. MOD SOURCE Self = the echo modulates its own time, Sine = the echo's own slow sine (0.23 Hz, not LFO 1 or 2; those go to Echo Time in the matrix).
TAPEHEAD 1 · HEAD 2 · WOWTape echo: one tape loop, two playback heads. Recording saturates and compresses, every playback loses highs and lows (120 Hz–2.8 kHz), so the repeats darken. WOW is the wobble of the tape speed (wow, flutter, a slow drift), DUST adds tape hiss. A new time makes the pitch slide like a tape speed change.
PING-PONGTIME L · TIME R · MODThe input goes into the left line only, left feeds right and right feeds left: the repeats bounce left and right.
LO-FITIME 1 · TIME 2 · DEGRADEA bad digital delay: the repeats lose sample rate (16 kHz down to ~3 kHz) and bits (12 down to 4), dark, grainy and a little noisy. DEGRADE sets how much.
MODTIME 1 · TIME 2 · DEPTHChorused repeats: each line swings by up to 8 ms, left and right a quarter apart. Clean and wide.
  • FEEDBACK: above ~55 % the echo starts to sing on its own. The loop has a ceiling, so the level never runs away. Tape and LO-FI hiss fade with the echo, so it falls silent in silence.
  • TO VOICES: the echo returns into the voices as FM. See GRAVITY.

MOTION

Movement in space. Switched on with ON; it sits after the filters and before ECHO and SPACE, so the echo and the tail travel with it. RATE 0.02–10 Hz, or with SYNC a DIVISION in tempo (locked to the song grid while the transport runs), DEPTH, a third knob per mode and MIX. RATE, DEPTH, X and MIX can all be modulated in the matrix.

MODEXWhat it does
ORBITWIDTHThe sound circles around the listener. The far ear hears it a little later (up to 0.65 ms) and darker, behind you it gets quieter and darker, and the changing distance bends the pitch slightly (Doppler). The stereo width of the input stays. Best on headphones.
PHASERFEEDBACKSix all-pass stages per side, the notches swing ±3 octaves around 400 Hz, the right side a quarter behind the left.
ENSEMBLESPREADThree copies swing slowly (RATE) and quickly (6.2 Hz) a third apart, like the chorus of old string machines. SPREAD places them across the stereo field.
TREMOLOSTEREOThe level pulses with a rounded sine. STEREO 0 = both sides together, 100 = the sides alternate.
ROTORSPEEDA rotating speaker: a horn above 800 Hz and a drum below, both swinging (Doppler and level) for two microphones. SPEED below the middle is slow, above it fast, and the rotors change speed with the inertia of the real thing (horn ~0.8 s, drum ~3.5 s). RATE is not used. Try the mod wheel on SPEED (the Rotor Organ preset).

SPACE

AlgorithmX · Y · ZSound
AURORAOCT UP · OCT DOWN · DECAYAiry shimmer. The attack is smeared and the lows are taken out, so the tail feels light. The octaves above and below are soft and keep drifting up or down as it fades. DECAY up to 20 s.
VOIDPRE-DELAY · WOBBLE · DECAYA huge dark space. DECAY at the very top freezes what is sounding (FREEZE).
RUNNERFEEDBACK · TIME · PITCHA staircase in space: the repeats bounce left and right, each one shifted by an interval. PITCH clicks into steps of −12, −7, −5, 0, +5, +7, +12 semitones and glides between them. The repeats melt into a small room.
SATURNFREQUENCY · STEPS · ROOMBells and bowls: a ring modulator (two sines an octave apart) sets three singing-bowl tones ringing. STEPS jumps by pentatonic notes with a soft glide, ROOM adds a space.
COMETFEEDBACK · TIME · WOWTape echo: every repeat saturates, darkens and loses its lows. WOW is the wow and flutter of the tape speed, DUST adds tape hiss. Up to 85 % FEEDBACK the repeats fade, above 85 % the loop sings, but never more than ~6 dB above its input.

Every loop in the FX has a ceiling and feedback compensation, and the stereo balance follows the input (measured within ±3 dB, L/R within 1 dB).

09The MOD page

All motion sources and all modulations live on one page (the MOD tab, key 4). Four sources at the top, the matrix below them.

SourceControls
LFO 1 and LFO 2SHAPE sine, triangle, saw up, saw down, square, S&H (a new random step every cycle). RATE 0.01–40 Hz, or with SYNC a DIVISION in tempo (8 bars to 1/16, triplets and dotted). While the transport runs it locks to the song grid. PHASE and RETRIG (every note restarts the LFO at PHASE).
RANDOMMODE: Drift (smooth wandering), Steps (random steps), Noise (fast jitter). RATE, or DIVISION with SYNC, SLEW smooths the steps.
MOD ENVAn ADSR envelope (ATTACK, DECAY, SUSTAIN, RELEASE, 1 ms–30 s). Every key you play starts it, plates and LEAD alike, and it fades after the last key is let go. Good for slowly opening a filter or bringing GRAVITY in with every chord.
LEAD ENVThe lead's filter envelope (pill in the FILTER ENV header on the LEAD page).

Modulation matrix

  • Drag a cell up or down to set the depth. In an empty cell this creates a new modulation.
  • Double-click removes it.
  • Right-click a cell for options: Enabled (switch off for now), Bipolar, Controller and its amount, Function and its amount, Remove.
  • + ADD DESTINATION adds a row with another destination. The menu follows the plug-in: voices 1–4 (the knobs of their current type on top), Gravity & Master, Lead, FX and Mod. At the very top is the last touched knob: turn a knob, click + ADD DESTINATION, done. The SPACE knobs are named after the chosen algorithm, e.g. "Space X (Pre-Delay)".
  • Sources (columns): LFO 1, LFO 2, Random, Mod Env, Lead Env, Velocity, Mod Wheel, Pressure (aftertouch), Pitch Bend, Constant. Up to 24 modulations.
  • Controller works like a VCA: the modulation is only as strong as a second source (e.g. LFO 1 → GRAVITY with Mod Wheel as controller = swings only as far as the wheel is up).
  • Function: Scale, Offset, Low / High Clip, Exponential, Norm Exponential, Low / High Pass, Slew, Low / High Slew, Sample & Hold, Bounce.

You can also add a modulation by dragging a source's pill onto a knob, or by right-clicking a knob → Modulate with. A modulated knob gets coloured rings, one per source. The ring under the mouse lights up; drag it to change its depth. With more than two modulations the knob shows +n.

10Playing and bars

OptionMeaning
PLAY: PlatesThe white keys C3, D3, E3, F3 play voices 1–4 at their own tuning. The pitch of the key doesn't matter, the touch does.
PLAY: KeysA played note retunes the next free voice to itself. With the LEAD on, every key plays the LEAD.
TOUCHWhat lifts a voice above HOLD: Velocity, Aftertouch (pressure after the strike, e.g. KeyStep) or Both.

The Help menu has the window size (70–150 %), tuning (A = 415 / 432 / 440 / 442 / 444 Hz), pitch bend range, plates position and silent preset changes (the output fades for 6 ms, echoes and old voices are cleared and the sound fades back in).

11Preset browser

  • Click the preset name in the top bar to open it (the arrows next to it step through presets without opening).
  • All / Factory / User / Favourites and the categories combine. Search covers name, category and description.
  • The star marks a favourite. The … menu: set as default (loads in every new instance), edit info, delete.
  • Save overwrites the current user preset, Save as stores a new one. An asterisk after the name at the top means the preset was changed.
  • ↑ ↓ load presets right away, Enter or Esc close the browser.

14Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
Silent after loadingThe preset has HOLD at 0 (Slow Bloom, Requiem Air…), or the DRONE is switched off (switch at the DRONE tab).
Keys outside C3–F3 do nothingThe LEAD is off. Switch it on at the LEAD tab.
The plates play the lead instead of the droneThe DRONE is off, so every key plays the LEAD. Switch the DRONE on.
A voice is silentIts TYPE is OFF, or VOL is at −∞.
FILTER B does nothingSwitch ON in FILTER B.
The LFO does nothingAn LFO changes nothing by itself: drag the coloured pill from its header onto a knob, or set a depth in the matrix on the MOD page.
PATH changes nothingPATH only matters from ORBIT up; in STILL the voices don't pull.
The arpeggio stopsARP only takes keys that play the LEAD. Without LATCH it plays only while you hold the keys. It follows the song tempo.
Aftertouch does nothingSet TOUCH to Aftertouch or Both. The keyboard must send channel pressure.
No tooltips over the knobsSwitch them on with the ? icon at the top right.
Whistling in the highsOversampling is 1× in the bottom bar, or CHARACTER is turned left (Digital).
Crackle and clicksThey come from TIDE above 50 % (the saws drop to a few Hz) and GRAVITY from ORBIT up. Turn GRIT in MASTER down to 30–60 %.
