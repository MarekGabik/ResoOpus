User manual · version 0.1.0

CSEN

ResoOpus

A drone synthesizer for cinematic, ambient and experimental soundscapes. Four hand-tuned, microtonal voices in two pairs play as soon as the plugin is on a track. In the centre sits the big GRAVITY knob, which leads from pure tuning to chaos. Add a filter with five characters, an echo, a space and a melodic LEAD layer with an arpeggiator.

VST3 · Windows / macOSAU · macOSinstrument · drone + mono leadAbleton Live 11 / 124 voices · 24 modulations · 42 presets

01How it works

ResoOpus is an instrument that plays by itself. Every voice has a HOLD level it sounds at without being touched. Playing a voice lifts it above that level, and letting go returns it there. The keys don't trigger the drone, they shape it.

PageContents
DRONEFour voices (type, tuning, HOLD, PAN, volume), GRAVITY, the selected voice's detail, MASTER (PITCH, DUST, VOLUME, FILL).
LEAD · FXAt the top FILTER, ECHO and SPACE for the whole instrument. Below the melodic LEAD layer (oscillators, envelope, vibrato) and ARP.
4 voices (CHOIR / SIREN / PULSAR)⇄GRAVITY · DUST→+ LEAD→FILTER→ECHO→SPACE→MASTER → limiter

The voices form two pairs, 1·2 and 3·4. GRAVITY lets the voices of a pair, and later all four in one ring, modulate each other. Voices, LEAD and filter run oversampled (2× by default), so high notes and heavy modulation stay clean. A soft limiter at the end keeps the output below −0.3 dBFS.

Silent? That's fine.Some presets (Slow Bloom, Requiem Air, Four Plates) have HOLD at zero and only sound while you play. All other presets drone right away.

02Quick start

  1. Put ResoOpus on a MIDI track in Ableton (Plug-Ins → Gavr → ResoOpus). You hear a drone immediately.
  2. Play the white keys C3, D3, E3, F3 (numbered 1–4 on the keyboard below). Each one swells one voice, more the harder you play.
  3. Turn GRAVITY in the middle. To the left: pure tuning and slow beating. To the right the voices start to tremble, growl and finally pulse on their own.
  4. Click the preset name at the top. The browser opens with categories and a description of what to turn first.
  5. On the LEAD · FX page try FILTER, ECHO and SPACE. The power icon at the tab switches the LEAD on: keys outside C3–F3 then play a melody.

03Voices

Every voice is its own module. Click it to select it and its details appear in the VOICE panel below.

  • The header shows the type and voice number (= plate number on the keyboard). The dot lights up while the voice sounds.
  • TUNE: C0–C8, free and microtonal. Double-click to type a note with cents, e.g. D2+12.
  • The second knob follows the type: TIDE (CHOIR), FM (SIREN), SHARP (PULSAR).
  • HOLD: drone level without touching anything. 0 % = only sounds while played.
  • PAN: position left / right.
  • The trace shows the voice's level over the last ~8 s. The rise is ATTACK, the fall RELEASE, the dashed line HOLD.
  • VOL: drag for volume (−60 to +6 dB), double-click = −2 dB. The thin green line below is the live level.

The voice detail always has TYPE (OFF / CHOIR / SIREN / PULSAR), ATTACK (1 ms–30 s, how slowly the voice swells, also when HOLD brings it in) and RELEASE (1 ms–30 s, how long it fades). A voice set to OFF uses no CPU.

04CHOIR · SIREN · PULSAR

CHOIR: five saws as a chord

  • CHORD fills all five intervals at once: Unison (a few cents apart, beating), Octaves, Fifths, Major (0 4 7 12 16), Minor (0 3 7 12 15).
  • OSC 1–5: interval of each saw above TUNE (−24 to +36 semitones). Shift-drag moves in cents. The LED above the knob switches the saw off.
  • TIDE (in the voice module): up to 50 % it pulls the whole chord down. Beyond 50 % it sinks further and the saws start to modulate each other: growl, metallic overtones and crackle at the bottom.

SIREN: sirens, birds, monsters, wind

ControlWhat it does
FM (voice module)How far the modulator sweeps the pitch, up to ±3 octaves.
RATEModulator speed 0.05–300 Hz: slow wails, warbles, buzzing. Above ~30 Hz it becomes a new timbre.
AMHow much the modulator chops the tone into beeps (Morse, alarm).
DIVIDERAdds a sub-harmonic: 1:2 = an octave below, 1:3, 1:5… up to 1:16. Growl and monsters.
NOISEWhite noise: wind, surf, static.
WAVEModulator shape: 0 = square (two-tone siren), 100 = triangle (wail).

PULSAR: one tone that blooms

  • SHARP (voice module): the shape of the tone from a soft triangle through shark fin and saw to a sharp square.
  • BLOOM: the envelope bends the tone. It starts a little flat and dull and blooms into pitch and edge.
  • TWIN and BLEND: a second tone above the first (−12 to +24 semitones). A few cents off unison = slow beating, 7 = a fifth.
  • SUB: a soft tone an octave below. VIBRATO: up to 50 cents; every voice has a slightly different speed, so they never wobble together.

  • CHOIR is five saws. CHORD only tunes them to different intervals. Their sum is jagged and changes every period because the saws aren't phase-locked. Unison "breathes" with slow beating. TIDE above 50 % lets every saw frequency-modulate the next (a ring of five): the regular teeth break up and it growls.
  • SIREN is a square with a slow modulator. FM sways the pitch up to ±3 octaves (WAVE: square = jumps between two tones, triangle = a smooth wail). AM chops the tone into beeps with 2 ms rounded edges. DIVIDER adds a second, lower square (1:2 to 1:16), hence the "steps". NOISE mixes in white noise.
  • PULSAR is one oscillator with a continuously variable shape. SHARP runs triangle → shark fin → saw → square, the in-betweens crossfade smoothly. TWIN adds a second tone of the same shape, SUB a soft tone an octave below. BLOOM dulls the shape as the voice swells in (up to 60 cents flat) and opens it with level.
  • LEAD uses the same shape oscillator but starts one step earlier, at a sine: WAVE 0–4 = sine → triangle → shark fin → saw → square. Two oscillators are mixed, OSC 2 can hard-sync to OSC 1 (SYNC).

All oscillators are band-limited: the jumps in saw and square are smoothed (BLEP), so there is no digital aliasing, and the voices run oversampled. Measured aliasing is −93 dB.

05GRAVITY

GRAVITY is the heart of the instrument: one knob from tuning to chaos. The voices frequency-modulate each other, first in pairs, then in one ring, and finally the instrument's own output joins the loop.

STILL 0–25 %Pure tuning, only slow beating between the voices.
ORBIT 25–55 %The voices of a pair pull on each other (FM), the tone trembles and glitters.
TURBULENCE 55–85 %All four in one loop, the output feeds back into the voices. It growls and breathes.
COLLAPSE 85–100 %The loop runs away and pulses on its own. It never repeats.

The coloured dots on the orbit are the voices in the colour of their type. They grow with their level, and as GRAVITY rises they pull together and shake. Loudness changes by only about 2 dB between STILL and COLLAPSE, so you can automate GRAVITY without jumps.

  • Right-click GRAVITY → PATH: Auto (pairs up to the middle, then a ring), Pairs (always pairs 1·2 and 3·4), Ring (always the ring 1→2→3→4→1). The menu also has Modulate with.
  • Drag up / down (Shift = fine), mouse wheel, double-click = type a value, Cmd/Ctrl-click = default 15 %.

When GRAVITY is modulated, a ring in the source's colour appears around the knob, with a dot for its live value. Drag the ring to change the depth.

The best pairing is finger pressure (aftertouch) on GRAVITY: a light touch keeps the voices in orbit, pressing harder pulls them into turbulence. The presets Gravity Well and Event Horizon work this way.

ECHO TO VOICES on the FX page feeds the echo back into the voices as extra FM. With more GRAVITY the whole instrument becomes one loop.

06MASTER and DUST

  • PITCH: transposes all four voices together (±24 semitones), their intervals stay the same.
  • DUST: analog life. Slow drift of the tuning, voices leaking into each other and a quiet breath of noise in the background (at most −54 dBFS). 0 % = clean digital.
  • VOLUME: output level (−∞ to +12 dB).
  • FILL (right in the header): spreads the four voices over a chosen scale from a chosen root. The quick way to a tuned chord.

07FILTER · ECHO · SPACE

FILTER

A stereo filter for the whole instrument, running oversampled. CUTOFF 20 Hz–20 kHz, RESONANCE 0–10, DRIVE 0–10, SPREAD detunes the left and right filters against each other (wider stereo). MODE: Low Pass, Band Pass, High Pass (High Pass removes the lows for clear, high drones).

ModelSlopeCharacter
GROWL12 dBDirty state-variable filter with saturating integrators. Loud input and resonance growl.
LADDER24 dBTransistor ladder: round, thins the lows a little at high resonance and sings.
DIODE24 dBDiode ladder with coupled stages: rubbery "squelch", the resonance bites more than it rings.
SCREAM12 dBClipped resonance loop: harsh, screams and screeches when pushed.
SILK12 dBClean and smooth, only the input is gently limited. For calm pads.

At full resonance Band Pass keeps its level (from +0.6 to +9.8 dB against resonance 0, depending on the model): a narrow band doesn't vanish, it sings.

ECHO

  • Two lines (TIME 1 left, TIME 2 right, 5 ms–2 s) with cross feedback. Time changes glide like tape.
  • FEEDBACK: above ~55 % the echo starts to sing on its own. You can "play" the standing waves with TIME. The loop has a ceiling, so the level never runs away.
  • MIX: dry / echo. MOD and MOD SOURCE: Self = the echo modulates its own time (resonances turn unstable), LFO = a slow sine.
  • TO VOICES: the echo returns into the voices as FM. See GRAVITY.

SPACE

Five algorithms. The labels of X, Y and Z follow the algorithm and a short description is right in the panel. BLEND is dry / effect.

AlgorithmX · Y · ZSound
AURORAOCT UP · OCT DOWN · DECAYShimmer: the reverb rises an octave up or falls an octave down as it decays.
VOIDPRE-DELAY · WOBBLE · DECAYA huge dark space. DECAY at the very top freezes what is sounding (FREEZE).
RUNNERFEEDBACK · TIME · PITCHRepeats that rise or fall. PITCH per repeat, middle = none.
SATURNFREQUENCY · STEPS · ROOMMetallic ring modulation. STEPS jumps to random notes, ROOM adds a space.
COMETFEEDBACK · TIME · WOBBLEA dirty, saturated delay that sings at high FEEDBACK.

Every loop in the FX has a ceiling and feedback compensation, and the stereo balance follows the input. Neither ECHO at 100 % nor COMET jumps in level or drifts to one side (measured within ±3 dB, L/R within 1 dB).

08LEAD and ARP

LEAD is a monophonic melodic layer above the drone. Switch it on with the power icon at the LEAD · FX tab (gold = playing). Then every key outside the plates C3–F3 plays the LEAD; in Keys mode all keys do. The LEAD goes through the same FILTER, ECHO and SPACE as the voices.

PanelControls
LEAD OSCWAVE morphs Sine → Triangle → Shark fin → Saw → Square. OSC 2 interval ±12 semitones (a few cents = chorus, 7 = fifth), OSC 2 LEVEL, OCTAVE ±2, SUB sine an octave below, SYNC = hard sync (set OSC 2 a few semitones up for the classic tear).
ENVELOPEADSR: ATTACK 1 ms–10 s, DECAY and RELEASE 1 ms–30 s, SUSTAIN.
LEAD VOICEGLIDE (slides between overlapping notes, 0–2 s), VIBRATO (100 % = a semitone), VIB RATE, VIB DELAY (vibrato fades in after each note), TOUCH VIB (more vibrato when pressing harder), VOLUME, PAN.
ARPARP switches the arpeggiator on, LATCH keeps it running after you let go. DIRECTION Up / Down / Ping-Pong / Random, RATE 1/4 to 1/32 including triplets, OCTAVES 1–4. Locked to the song tempo.

The last pressed note always plays. When you release it, the LEAD returns to the previously held note.

09Modulation

ResoOpus has 24 modulation slots. Each one runs source → function → controller → amount → destination. Any continuous knob of the voices, GRAVITY, FX and LEAD can be a destination.

  • Right-click a knob → Modulate with → source.
  • In the Mod panel (top right or key 9) with + Add.
  • Sources: Velocity, Mod Wheel, Pressure (aftertouch), Pitch Bend, Constant.
  • Controller works like a VCA: the modulation is only as strong as a second source (e.g. velocity → TIDE with Mod Wheel as controller).
  • Function: Scale, Offset, Low / High Clip, Exponential, Norm Exponential, Low / High Pass, Slew, Low / High Slew, Sample & Hold, Bounce.
  • Bipolar: on = swings around the knob position, off = one direction only.

10Playing and bars

OptionMeaning
PLAY: PlatesThe white keys C3, D3, E3, F3 play voices 1–4 at their own tuning. The pitch of the key doesn't matter, the touch does.
PLAY: KeysA played note retunes the next free voice to itself. A helper for chromatic playing.
TOUCHWhat lifts a voice above HOLD: Velocity (how hard you strike), Aftertouch (pressure after the strike, e.g. KeyStep) or Both.
Snap (bottom bar)Free = microtonal, Chromatic = voices snap to semitones, Scale = to a scale. Scales: Major, Minor, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Harmonic Minor, Pentatonic Major/Minor, Whole Tone, Hirajoshi, Double Harmonic.

The Help menu has the window size (70–150 %), tuning (A = 415 / 432 / 440 / 442 / 444 Hz), pitch bend range, plates position (from C1 / C2 / C3 / C4) and silent preset changes. Silent preset changes (on by default) fade the output for 6 ms, clear echoes and old voices and fade back in.

11Preset browser

  • Click the preset name in the top bar to open it (the arrows next to it step through presets without opening).
  • All / Factory / User / Favourites and the categories combine. Search covers name, category and description.
  • The star marks a favourite. The … menu: set as default (loads in every new instance), edit info, delete.
  • Save overwrites the current user preset, Save as stores a new one (name, category, description). An asterisk after the name at the top means the preset was changed.
  • ↑ ↓ load presets right away, Enter or Esc close the browser. The ☰ menu: open a file, show the folder, rescan.
